Tim Goedhart
While leaving my house, this tiny fella was sitting on the glass door of my building. As quick as I could, I got my camera out of my bag, aimed, shot and went on to catch my train. Later, when I was editing the photo, it got to me that it looks a lot like he/she is doing the wave. The crop makes the background look grainy and the dust on the glass door finishes of the vintage look of the picture. This is what I call a lucky moment!
